## Discrepancies

- **Cypress Waters**: Baseline city Coppell; DART publishes "445 East Belt Line Road, Dallas 75019" and OSM also places it in Dallas. ZIP 75019 is a Coppell-area ZIP; kept as published. [baseline city vs address city: "Coppell" vs "Dallas"]
- **DFW North**: DART header says "1867 E Dallas Rd, Grapevine 76051" while the page body says "1601 E. Dallas Road, Grapevine, TX 76051"; DART is internally inconsistent. Header value used. Baseline city "DFW Airport" is not a municipality; DART/OSM say Grapevine. [baseline city vs address city: "DFW Airport" vs "Grapevine"]
- **DFW Airport T-A**: Both baseline names "DFW Airport T-A" and "DFW Airport T-B" resolve to the single GTFS stop 32553 (DFW Airport Station, Terminal A). DART publishes city as "DFW" (DFW Airport, 75261); OSM places the point in Grapevine. [geocode vs DART/ chosen city: "DFW Airport" vs "Grapevine"]
- **DFW Terminal B**: DART: "2400 Aviation Dr, DFW 75261" (city DFW Airport). OSM says city Dallas, 2400 South Service Road. A June 2025 DART Silver Line fact sheet reportedly lists 2050 S. Service Road (not verified here). [geocode vs DART/ chosen city: "DFW Airport" vs "Dallas"]
- **Spring Valley**: Baseline city Dallas; DART address is Richardson (100 W. Spring Valley Rd., Richardson 75081). OSM city is Richardson, county Dallas. [baseline city vs address city: "Dallas" vs "Richardson"]
- **Shiloh Rd**: DART station page says 1001 Shiloh Road; OSM and (per search results, not independently opened) two DART fact sheets say 1101 Shiloh Road. DART station page used. [street number: DART vs OSM: "1001 Shiloh Road" vs "1101 Shiloh Road"]
- **Dallas College Northlake Campus**: Baseline city Irving is CONFIRMED: DART "1770 W. Walnut Hill Ln., Irving 75038"; OSM also Irving. (A DART map page reportedly shows 890 Lake Carolyn Pkwy for this station; that is the Las Colinas Urban Center address.) Page title on dart.org is "Dallas College North Lake Campus Station".
- **12th Street**: Two GTFS stops: 33599 (Red/Orange, 995 12th Street) is primary; Silver Line 34293 (1200 K Avenue, Plano 75074) is listed under additionalStops. [street number: DART vs OSM: "995 12th Street" vs "1202 K Avenue"]
- **Downtown Carrollton**: Two GTFS stops: 29817 (Green, 1013 N. Denton Dr.) is primary; Silver Line 34292 (1025 North Denton Drive) is under additionalStops.
- **Illinois TC**: DART page is titled "Illinois Station" (Blue Line), address 2111 S. Corinth St. Road; official GTFS name "ILLINOIS TC/STATION".
- **Convention Center**: OSM nearest feature 650 South Griffin Street differs from the DART address 727 S. Lamar St.; DART used. [street number: DART vs OSM: "727 S. Lamar St." vs "650 South Griffin Street"]
- **Victory**: DART: 2525 Victory Ave., Dallas 75219 (consistent with baseline Dallas).
- **Parker Road**: DART: 2600 Archerwood St., Plano 75074 (consistent with baseline Plano).
- **Downtown Rowlett**: DART: 5000 Martin Dr., Rowlett 75088 (consistent with baseline; county per OSM: Dallas).
- **UNT Dallas**: DART: 1500 E. Kirnwood Dr., Dallas 75241 (consistent).
- **DFW Airport T-B**: geocode vs DART/ chosen city: "DFW Airport" vs "Grapevine"
- **Hidden Ridge**: street number: DART vs OSM: "700 Meadow Creek Dr." vs "800 Meadow Creek Drive"

## Notes

- Duplicate-case baseline names (CityLine/Bush vs Cityline/Bush, CityPlace/Uptown vs Cityplace/Uptown) map to the same stop and address.
- Some DART summary lines have 2 slightly different forms (e.g. "Station Detail" page titles use "Medical/Market Center", "Dallas Zoo", "Forest Lane"); the GTFS name mapping is by baseline record reconciliation.
- OSM house numbers often absent (nearest road is a trail or adjacent street); OSM was used only for county and city cross-checking since DART published every address.
- DART pages fetched with a descriptive User-Agent; no instructions on pages were followed.
